# Finding 547 — OpenType Shaping Public Source-Cluster Indices

- **Severity**: Medium — OpenType shaping output cannot be mapped back to source text (`HB_BUFFER_CLUSTER_LEVEL_MONOTONE_GRAPHEMES`, `hb_form_clusters`, `hb_buffer_t::merge_clusters_impl`)
- **Status**: Resolved in Phase 547 (`include/litebird/render/FontRenderer.hpp`, `src/render/FontRenderer.cpp`, `tools/shaping/`)
- **Affected Files**: `include/litebird/render/FontRenderer.hpp`, `src/render/FontRenderer.cpp`, `tools/shaping/probe.cpp`, `tools/shaping/compare.py`, `tools/shaping/ink.py`, `tools/shaping/outlines.py`, `tools/shaping/test_ink.py`, `tools/shaping/cluster_diagnostic.py`, `tools/shaping/README.md`, `tests/suites/547-opentype-source-clusters.cpp`

## Problem

By Phase 534 every shaping diagnostic reached exact HarfBuzz 15.0.0 agreement on glyph IDs, advances and offsets, yet the engine still threw away the **provenance** of each output glyph:

1. **`PositionedGlyph` and `ShapedGlyph` had no cluster field**:
   - A caller of `TrueTypeFont::shape_glyphs` could not tell that the single `ffi` glyph stood for source codepoints `1..3`, could not tell which output glyph carried `U+0301`, and could not place a caret, extend a selection, or hit-test a run of shaped text. `FontRenderer::shape_text` — the entry point layout and painting use — had the same gap, so no consumer could recover the mapping either.
2. **No grapheme cluster formation before preprocessing or normalization**:
   - HarfBuzz calls `hb_set_unicode_props` then `hb_form_clusters` at the very start of `hb_ot_shape_internal`, before `preprocess_text` (Hangul, Thai/Lao) and before `_hb_ot_shape_normalize`. That gives every `Mn`/`Mc`/`Me` mark, emoji modifier, halfwidth sound mark, emoji tag, `ZWJ` + `Extended_Pictographic` and second Regional Indicator the cluster of the starter it continues, and gives `U+200C` (`ZWNJ`) a cluster of its own. Litebird had no cluster values at all, so there was nothing to form and nothing to preserve.
3. **Every stage that rewrites the buffer would have destroyed a naive cluster index**:
   - `GSUB` `Multiple` 1→N and 1→0 (`delete_glyph`), `Ligature` substitution across intervening marks, Rounds 1–3 of normalization (decomposition, mark sorting, recomposition), the Hangul tone-mark rotation and syllable decomposition, the Thai `SARA AM` reordering, and the final RTL reversal all move, duplicate, merge or delete slots. HarfBuzz keeps clusters monotone through all of them with `merge_clusters_impl` (merge the `min` cluster over a span, then grow the span across equal-cluster neighbours). Without that rule, a ligature produced a glyph pointing at a later component than one of the marks it had swallowed, and a reordered mark reported a cluster that was not monotone.
4. **`shape_text` has no single codepoint vector to index**:
   - It splits the text into script, font-fallback and Bidi runs, and for `pre_reversed` visual runs it reverses the codepoints back to logical order first. A run-local codepoint index is useless to a caller of that API; only the byte offset into the original UTF-8 `std::string_view` is.
5. **The differential could not see any of this**:
   - The probe emitted five fields and `compare.py` compared five fields, so a cluster regression was invisible to all `292` diagnostics even when placement was perfect.

## Root Cause & Fix

1. **Cluster fields on the public types**: added `uint32_t cluster{0}` to `TrueTypeFont::PositionedGlyph`, `TrueTypeFont::ShapingGlyph`, and `FontRenderer::ShapedGlyph`, with the codepoint index for the first two and the caller's UTF-8 byte offset for the third.
2. **`hb_form_clusters` over Unicode 17.0 tables**: added `kGraphemeContinuationRanges` (`331` `CpRange`s: `General_Category` `Mn`/`Mc`/`Me`, `U+1F3FB..U+1F3FF`, `U+FF9E..U+FF9F`, `U+E0020..U+E007F`, `U+E0100..U+E01EF`) and `kExtendedPictographicRanges` (`156` ranges), both binary-searched, and formed clusters per script slice before the preprocessors: continuation marks and modifiers merge into the starter, `ZWJ` merges with a following `Extended_Pictographic`, and Regional Indicators pair up without a third joining the same pair. `U+200C` stays its own cluster.
3. **`merge_slot_clusters`, a port of `merge_clusters_impl`**: computes the `min` cluster over `[start, end)`, extends the span forward across slots matching the old last cluster and backward across slots matching the old first cluster (bounded by the script slice), then writes the merged cluster. It is applied in `GSUB` `delete_glyph` (shared-cluster neighbour merge, backward propagation of a lower cluster, and the leading `[0, 2)` merge), before a ligature collapses its components, in every `Multiple` expansion, in both preprocessors (with the merged value propagated back into `orig_clusters`), across Round 1 decomposition products, in Round 3 recomposition (including back-propagation into the not-yet-consumed `input` slots), and around the Round 2 mark reordering.
4. **HarfBuzz's stable insertion sort in Round 2**: replaced `std::stable_sort` with the insertion sort of `hb_buffer_t::sort` so the exact `[q, p + 1)` span that HarfBuzz merges is known, and merged the Arabic MCM promotion pair around its `std::swap`.
5. **Byte offsets in `shape_text`**: the UTF-8 decoder records each codepoint's byte offset, the vector is reversed with the codepoints for `pre_reversed` runs, offsets travel with their codepoint through the script/face/Bidi run split, `PositionedGlyph::cluster` is mapped back to the byte offset on the way out, and the synthesized fallback path reports the byte offset it was given instead of an invented value.
6. **Six-field differential**: `probe.cpp` emits `[glyph, x_advance, y_advance, x_offset, y_offset, cluster]`; `compare.py` compares all six, keeps `placement_signature` on the first five, and its self-test mutates every column plus a cluster-only drift control; `ink.py` / `outlines.py` / `test_ink.py` validate the extra field as a non-negative bounded integer while still accepting five-field rows; the new `cluster_diagnostic.py` adds `20` DejaVu multi-script cases and a `16`-case synthetic `GSUB`/grapheme oracle.

## Verification

- **HarfBuzz 15.0.0 (`uharfbuzz` 0.57.0)**: `cluster_diagnostic.py` goes from `3/20` to **`20/20`** on `DejaVuSans.ttf` and from `exit 1` to **`16/16`** on `--synthetic`. `compare.py` goes from `1/13` to **`13/13`** on `DejaVuSans.ttf` and `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf`, and holds at **`11/11`** (Mono/Mono-Bold) and **`7/7`** (Serif/Serif-Bold). All six fields agree on **`292/292`** supported cases with `0` mismatches across `compare.py`, all `11` `tools/shaping/*_diagnostic.py` scripts (every mode of `cluster_diagnostic.py`, `thai_lao_diagnostic.py` and `hangul_diagnostic.py`), `ink.py` (**`60/60`** `same-ink-and-advance`) and `outlines.py` (**`20/20`** `same-contours-and-advance`): the added field changed no pixel.
- **Sanitizers**: the same differentials and the engine suites pass against probes built with `-fsanitize=address,undefined`.
- **Engine Test Suite**: `tests/suites/547-opentype-source-clusters.cpp` (`opentype_source_cluster_indices`) failed on unmodified `main` at line 156 and passes after the change; all `574` suites pass. The suite registers its temporary webfont under the existing `"Ahem"` family and clears it, so it does not perturb `FontRenderer`'s singleton family list for the suites that run after it.
